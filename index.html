About

DJ Rossi Wine

DJ Rossi Wine is one of Uganda's most electrifying and talented disc jockeys, known for his seamless blends, crowd-reading mastery, and ability to keep any dance floor on fire from start to finish.

Born and raised in Uganda, Rossi Wine has carved out a distinct sonic identity that fuses Afrobeats, Amapiano, Dancehall, Hip-Hop, R&B, and the rich sounds of East Africa into explosive mixes that resonate across the continent and beyond.

With years of experience performing at top clubs, corporate events, concerts, and private parties, DJ Rossi Wine has built a reputation as a professional whose sets leave audiences wanting more — every single time.

"The music doesn't stop. The vibe never dies."
